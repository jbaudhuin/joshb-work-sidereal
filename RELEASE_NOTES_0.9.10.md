# Zodiac Sidereal 0.9.10

**Released:** 2026-10-13

- Primary directions in the Events tab: Placidus, Campanus and Regiomontanus systems, direct/converse, optional rapt parallels, and a focal Directions preview for a clicked direction
- Reworked directed chart: natal bodies held fixed while the mundane frame rotates; derivation state saved with charts and sessions
- Mundane-mode star overlays: stars on the anchoring chart's angles, and heliacal rising/setting stars by the dawn method (method after Bernadette Brady), with an editable curated star list
- Fixed-star labels: constellation + Greek-letter tooltips and zodiacal sign glyphs across the tables, Speculum dock and chart; full star names in exports
- Plain report search with quoted phrases; separate Solar/Lunar/Planetary return filters; Harmonic vs named aspect sets
- Fixes: BC dates save/load, duplicate events on session restore, duplicate/early return events, duration filter dropping returns, missing closest-approach transits to natal/progressed Moon, circumpolar-star NaN times

Acknowledgements: quotidian directions (PSSR, SQ, NeoPSSR, NeoSQ) draw on the work of Cyril Fagan and Kenneth Bowser; primary directions follow Bob Makransky's *Primary Directions: A Primer of Calculation*; the dawn-method heliacal stars follow Bernadette Brady.
