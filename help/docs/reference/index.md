# Reference

The pages in this section are the ones you reach for to look something up rather
than to learn a feature — the settings, the switches, and what to do when
something goes wrong.

- **[Settings Reference](settings.md)** — a walk through the Settings dialog and
    its tabs: General, Chart, Tables, Events, and Harmonics.
- **[Themes](themes.md)** — the Dark, Light, and Printable themes and how they
    affect the whole interface.
- **[Command-Line Options](command-line.md)** — flags for starting a fresh
    session or loading a specific one, and for running more than one instance.
- **[Troubleshooting &amp; Crash Reports](troubleshooting.md)** — common setup snags,
    where your files live, and how crash reports are gathered.
- **[Credits &amp; License](credits.md)** — the people, libraries, and license behind
    Zodiac.
