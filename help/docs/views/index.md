# The Views

The same chart can be shown in more than one way. Zodiac gives you four
**views** of a single open chart, and you switch between them freely without
recasting anything: a classical **chart wheel**, a **Planets view**, a **Tables
(text) view**, and a **Details view**. Each shows the same underlying data from a
different angle — a picture, a focused look at the planets, plain text tables, or
detailed properties of one element.

Crucially, the display options that shape what you see — the **harmonic**, the
**house system**, the **zodiac**, the **aspect set**, and the **aspect mode**
(ecliptic, prime vertical, equatorial, or great-circle) — are treated as
**view-level settings**, not properties of the chart file. Switching them changes
what is drawn without marking the chart dirty, so you can experiment with display
options and never be prompted to save a change you did not really make.

## The four views

- **[The Chart Wheel](chart-wheel.md)** — the default view; a classical wheel of
    signs, houses, planets, and aspect lines.
- **[Planets Panel](planets.md)** — a dockable table of every body's position
    for one or two charts, with a search that finds a chart from typed positions.
- **[Tables (Text) View](tables.md)** — the chart's information as text tables in
    lieu of the wheel.
- **[Details](details.md)** — detailed properties and interpretations for a
    selected element.

The wheel has many capabilities of its own; those are gathered in
[The Chart Wheel in Depth](../chart-wheel/index.md).
