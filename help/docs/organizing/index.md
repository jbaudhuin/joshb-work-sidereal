# Organizing Your Work

Once you have more than a handful of charts, two features keep everything tidy:
the **chart database** and **sessions**. Together they mean you never have to
hunt for a chart or rebuild your workspace by hand.

- **[The Chart Database](database.md)** — a folder tree for your chart files.
      Group charts into folders and subfolders, move and copy them around, and reach
      the ones you used most recently. Because the files live in your Documents
      folder, they travel with any cloud-storage service you already use.

- **[Sessions &amp; Multi-Window](sessions.md)** — Zodiac remembers the tabs, views,
      and layout you had open and restores them next time. You can name a session to
      keep a line of research separate, and run more than one session at once.
